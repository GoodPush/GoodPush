# Code of conduct

GoodPush's issues and pull requests are a place where people share their training, and sometimes their health.
Everyone is welcome: beginners and racers, people who code and people who don't.

* **Be kind and patient.** Assume good faith; explain rather than correct.
* **Respect people's data.** Never repost someone's numbers, screenshots or health details elsewhere, and never
  ask anyone to share more than they offered.
* **No medical or training verdicts on other people.** Share what worked for you; don't tell others what's wrong
  with them.
* **No harassment**, personal attacks, discrimination or unwelcome attention of any kind.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, and block people, who don't follow this. If something here
bothers you, contact the maintainer through [GoodPush on GitHub](https://github.com/GoodPush) rather than in a
public thread.
