# Security

GoodPush runs on your computer and keeps your sign-ins in your user folder (`~/.goodpush/`). Parts of it can listen
on your network: the app with `goodpush app --lan`, and pairing with your other computers.

If you find a way for someone else to reach your data, your sign-ins or your computer through GoodPush, please
**don't open a public issue**. Report it privately instead:
[Security > Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/GoodPush/GoodPush/security/advisories/new) on GitHub. You'll
get an answer as soon as the maintainer can; it's a spare-time project, so please allow a few days.

Only the latest release gets security fixes.
