# Help and feedback

GoodPush is in beta: your reports are how it gets better. All of these need a free
[GitHub account](https://github.com/signup), and everything on GitHub issues is **public**: never attach ride files
or exports, and check screenshots for your name, maps and health numbers.

## Something's wrong

In the app: **Beta** (top right) > **Report a problem**. It opens GitHub's form with your GoodPush version and type of
computer filled in. Or [report it here](https://github.com/GoodPush/GoodPush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
Say what you did, step by step, what you expected and what happened instead.

## Do the numbers make sense?

GoodPush's model of your physiology has been tested on very few people. If you know something about your body that
you trust (a lab test, a ramp or 20-minute test, a race, confirmed heart-rate zones, or simply how sessions feel),
compare it with what GoodPush shows on the **Rider** page and
[tell us](https://github.com/GoodPush/GoodPush/issues/new?template=numbers.yml), whether it matches or not.
Relative numbers are enough ("about 10 % below my lab LT1"). What helps most:
[Does the model work for you?](contributing.md#does-the-model-work-for-you)

## Ideas and questions

[Idea or question](https://github.com/GoodPush/GoodPush/issues/new?template=idea.yml): say what you'd like to do and
why. Confusing wording counts: quote it and say what you thought it meant.

## A security problem

Please don't post it publicly: [report it privately](https://github.com/GoodPush/GoodPush/security/advisories/new).
