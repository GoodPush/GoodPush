# GoodPush

**Free cycling-training analysis and an adaptive training plan that run on your own computer.** Connect your Garmin
(or drop in a Strava or Garmin export) and GoodPush shows what you can do now, how your training has gone, how your
body is doing, and what to ride next. Everything runs locally: your data never leaves your computer.

:::{admonition} Beta: expect bugs
:class: warning

GoodPush is in beta testing. Things will break, numbers can be off, and settings may change between versions. Please
[tell us what goes wrong](https://github.com/GoodPush/GoodPush/issues/new/choose), and whether
[the numbers make sense for you](community/feedback.md#do-the-numbers-make-sense). GoodPush is not medical advice:
see the [disclaimer](https://github.com/GoodPush/GoodPush/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.md).
:::

**New here?** [Install it](start/install.md), then [get your data in](start/data.md). The rest of the guide explains
each page of the app; you don't need to read it all.

**Want to help?** Most of the help GoodPush needs is no code at all: [using it and saying what goes
wrong](community/feedback.md), and saying whether its numbers match what you know about your body. See
[Contributing](community/contributing.md). GoodPush is free; [What it costs](community/costs.md) lists what would
cost money, such as code signing and app stores.
