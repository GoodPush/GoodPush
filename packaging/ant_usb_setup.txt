goodpush: setting up your ANT+ USB stick (Windows, once per computer)
=====================================================================

goodpush talks to your trainer, power meter and heart-rate strap through an ANT+ USB stick. Windows needs a
small USB driver for the stick (WinUSB). Zadig, the program that opened next to this note, puts it on.

Before you start
- Plug the stick in.
- Quit Zwift, Incyclist, TrainerRoad, Garmin Express and any other app that uses the stick.

1. Zadig asks Windows for permission: click Yes. If it asks whether to check for updates, either answer is fine.

2. In Zadig's menu: Options > List All Devices (so it has a tick).

3. Open the drop-down list at the top and pick your stick. It is usually called "ANT USBStick2",
   "ANT USB-m Stick" or "Dynastream ...".
   To be sure it's the stick: the first "USB ID" box below the list says 0FCF.
   No entry with 0FCF? Unplug the stick, plug it back in, and pick Options > List All Devices again.

   CAREFUL: pick only the stick. Changing the driver of anything else (keyboard, mouse, Bluetooth, webcam,
   headset) can stop that device working.

4. Look at the "Driver" box to the LEFT of the green arrow: that is what the stick has now.
   - It says WinUSB or libusb0: the stick is already set up (Zwift or Incyclist may have done it).
     Change nothing: close Zadig and go to "Check" below.
   - Anything else, or empty: make sure the box to the RIGHT of the arrow says WinUSB (the small up / down
     arrows next to it change it), then click "Install Driver" (or "Replace Driver").

5. Wait until Zadig says the driver was installed successfully. It can take a minute or two.

6. Close Zadig. Unplug the stick and plug it back in.

Check
Start a ride in goodpush. The first time, it lists the trainers, power meters, cadence sensors and heart-rate
straps it hears: pick yours. If it says "No ANT+ USB stick found", go through the steps again.
Only one app can use the stick at a time: quit the others before you ride with goodpush.

To do this again later: Start menu > goodpush ANT+ USB setup.

Undo (if another app no longer finds the stick)
Device Manager > find the stick (under "Universal Serial Bus devices") > right-click > Uninstall device,
tick "Delete the driver software for this device", then unplug the stick and plug it back in.
